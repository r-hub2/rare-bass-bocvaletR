bank-of-canada-api

Introduction

This vignette demonstrates how to retrieve and visualize financial time series data from the Bank of Canada using the bocvaletR package. Retrieve exchange rate data We retrieve the USD/CAD exchange rate from the Bank of Canada Valet API.

library(bocvaletR)
df <- boc_series(
"FXUSDCAD",
start_date = "2024-01-01",
end_date   = "2024-03-31"
)

Financial interpretation

The USD/CAD exchange rate reflects the relative value of the U.S. dollar against the Canadian dollar and is widely used in international trade, monetary policy analysis, and risk management.
